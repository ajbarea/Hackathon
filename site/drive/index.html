Drive it¶

You need the Myo armband, conda and Chrome. Quit Myo Connect first if it is running.

git clone https://github.com/ajbarea/Hackathon.git && cd Hackathon
conda env create -f environment.yml          # once, about 5 minutes
conda activate jackal                        # in every new terminal
python examples/lever.py <your name>

That one command:

  1. Connects to the Myo. On a Mac, allow Bluetooth for your terminal if asked.
  2. Calibrates you, the first time only (about 2.5 minutes). Put the band on snugly, on the thickest part of your forearm, and wait 2 minutes for it to warm up. Then hold six poses as it prompts you. Your calibration is saved in data/<your name>-lever.
  3. Opens the sim in your browser and drives. Pick a course (bottom right). Hold your arm forward and level for the countdown, then raise it once to start.
  4. Ctrl+C ends a drive. Then press Enter to drive again, r to recalibrate, t to tune the turns, or q to quit.

Next time, the same command skips straight to driving. Recalibrate whenever the band comes off.

How you drive¶

Your arm The robot
Forward, level Goes. Lower your arm to go faster
Raised from the elbow Stops
Rolled left or right Turns
Tipped down Reverses
Firm fist, held 0.4 s Brakes

Relaxing your hand never moves the robot, so driving is not tiring.

If something goes wrong¶

What you see Fix
CondaError: Run 'conda init' Run conda init zsh once (or conda init bash), then open a new terminal
No Myo found, or stuck connecting Plug the Myo into USB for a few seconds to wake it, unplug it, and move it. Close Myo Connect
Found once, then gone It fell asleep: unplug it, put it on and keep moving while it connects
Still not found Another laptop may hold it: stop lever.py there, then run python examples/myo_check.py

More settings (--expo, --vmax, --no-assist, --no-lag-comp) and tuning are in examples/MYO_LEVER.md.

No armband?¶

Open the live sim and drive with W, A, S and D to see the courses. To try the signal pipeline, python examples/fake_myo_lsl.py publishes the same streams with synthetic data.
